# Deliverable 2a: Interview kit

Owner: Kundan Singh (requirements lead). Every team member takes part in at least one interview.

## Who we will interview (target: at least 4 real people)

| Planned ID | Role | How we reach them |
|---|---|---|
| INT-01 | Community manager (management company, several HOAs) | Through Kundan, who works with this manager as HOA board VP |
| INT-02 | HOA board member (not a team member) | Kundan's board, a colleague on it |
| INT-03 | Resident / homeowner who has sent a maintenance request in the last year | Referral from INT-01 or INT-02 |
| INT-04 | Second community manager, or a management-company admin | Referral from INT-01 ("who else should we talk to?") |
| INT-05 (stretch) | Tenant renting a unit (the stakeholder who is easy to miss) | Referral from INT-03 |

Teammates, other CS 690 teams, AI models and synthetic users do **not** count toward the four.
Kundan cannot be an interviewee for his own team. Interviewees must be 18 or older.

## Opening script (read at the start of every interview)

> Thanks for making time. We are graduate students at NJIT, and this is a class project on how
> HOAs and their management companies handle requests, documents, notices and dues. We'll take
> notes. We will not write down your name or your community's name; notes refer to you only by
> a number and your role. The notes are used only for our class project. You can skip any question
> or stop at any time. *(If recording or using an AI transcriber:)* May we record this / run the
> recording through an AI transcription tool? We'll delete the recording after we have checked the notes.

If the conversation turns to something the person would not want written down, stop taking
notes and say so.

## Rules for the interviewer (Mom Test)

- Talk about their life, not our idea. Do not describe CommunityConnect or show any screen.
- Ask about specific past events ("the last time…"), not opinions about the future.
- Listen more than you talk. Ask "what happened next?" and "how did you find out?"
- If they pitch a feature or compliment the idea, write it on the compliments and predictions list, then steer back to a past event.

## Questions: community manager (revised from proposal Appendix C)

Open with their situation:

1. Tell me about your job: how many communities do you look after right now, and what does a normal week look like?

Dig into real events (★ = asks about a specific past event):

2. ★ Tell me about the last maintenance request a resident sent you. How did it reach you, and what happened between then and the day it was closed?
3. ★ Was there a time in the last few months when a resident asked you for an update on a request? What did they ask, and how did you find the answer?
4. ★ Think of the last request that was "fixed" and then came back. How did you find out it wasn't fixed, and what did you do?
5. ★ Walk me through the last notice you sent to a whole community. What did you do, step by step, and how did you find out who had seen it?
6. ★ Have you ever had to send the same notice to more than one of your communities? Tell me about the last time.
7. ★ When did a board member last ask you for a document? Where was it, and how long did it take to get it to them?
8. ★ Has a document or message ever reached someone who should not have seen it, for example a board-only file reaching a resident, or one community's information reaching another? What happened the last time?
9. ★ Think of last month's dues for one of your communities. How did the payments come in, and what did you do about the ones that were late or missing?
10. ★ Tell me about the last late payment you followed up on. How many days after the due date was that, and what did you send?
11. ★ The last time a tenant (not the owner) reported a problem, how did it reach you and what happened?

Close:

12. Is there anything about how you handle these that we should have asked about and didn't?
13. Who else should we talk to? (another manager, a board member, a resident, the person who sets up accounts at your company)

## Questions: board member

1. Tell me about your role on the board and how often you deal with the management company.
2. ★ When did you last need a board document (minutes, a contract, a bid)? Where did you get it, and how long did it take?
3. ★ Has a board-only document ever been seen by someone outside the board? What happened?
4. ★ Tell me about the last announcement the board wanted sent to residents. Who wrote it, who sent it, and how did you know residents got it?
5. ★ Tell me about the last time a resident complained to you directly about a request that wasn't handled. What happened next?
6. ★ Think of the last time the board reviewed late or missing dues. What information did you have, and where did it come from?
7. Who else should we talk to?

## Questions: resident

1. How long have you lived in the community, and how do you usually contact the HOA or management company?
2. ★ Tell me about the last time you reported a problem in the community. How did you report it, and how did you find out what was happening with it?
3. ★ Has a problem you reported ever been marked fixed when it wasn't? What did you do?
4. ★ Think of how you paid your last HOA dues. Walk me through what you did.
5. ★ The last time you missed or were late with a dues payment, how did you find out and what happened?
6. ★ The last notice you got from the HOA: how did it reach you, and what did you do with it?
7. Who else should we talk to?

Each role's list has at least five ★ questions about a specific past event.
