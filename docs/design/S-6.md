# S-6 Scheduled monthly dues payment: design doc

## 1. Problem

Most late payers simply forgot (E-21), and paying by two channels caused a double payment that took three weeks to refund (E-9). Residents need one scheduled payment that can never be charged twice for a month.

## 2. Scope

- A resident schedules an automatic payment for their unit (method, test token, day of the month), and the resident and manager see the unit’s payment history.
- A daily run charges each scheduled `debit` or `credit` unit once a month through Stripe test mode, retrying a declined charge after RETRY_DELAY_DAYS, up to MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS attempts. `ach` schedules are created and shown but are not charged automatically (settled OQ2, D34).

**Implementation status:** AC-18 to AC-21 are implemented and their tests run. AC-22 (retries under R5, D17 and D35, only within the same billing month) is implemented as proposed by the AGENT-03 AC-22 patch, and its acceptance test is no longer skipped; whether the tests pass is established only by a human test run recorded in LEDGER.md, not by this document. AGENT-03 remediations F-01 (a scheduled attempt, including a retry, and a manual payment for the same unit are serialized by a unit-row lock in READ COMMITTED transactions), F-02 (a month with any `Paid` Payment is skipped), F-03 (a stronger crash-recovery test) and F-11 (a completed Payment is never overwritten, and a conflicting processor result stops the run) are part of the implementation, under the single-runner limitation of D32 and the limits in the Step 1 review clarification below. AC-18 verifies ACH schedule creation and display, not settlement; automatic ACH charging is not built, under the settled OQ2 decision (D34). Per D34, AC-19 to AC-22 exercise debit schedules with `FakeProcessor`; passing tests do not validate ACH settlement or live payments. `StripeTestProcessor` is a placeholder that raises `ProcessorUnavailable`, and `python -m app.run_payments` (D19, D33) is not built, so no live Stripe processing has been verified.

## 3. Non-goals

- No real money (Stripe test mode only), no overdue flags or reminders (S-7), and no dues report (S-8).
- No changing, pausing or cancelling a schedule, and no partial payments, late fees or card fees (OQ1).

## 6. Rules

- **R1 (invariant):** For every unit and billing month there is at most one Payment with status `Paid`, whatever its method.
- **R2 (invariant):** No two Payments share an idempotency key, and each processor call uses the key of the Payment it records.
- **R3 (precondition):** `run_due_payments` creates no Payment for a unit that already has a `Paid` Payment for the month, including a `manual` one.
- **R4 (postcondition):** After `run_due_payments(today)`, every due `debit` or `credit` schedule (D18, D25) whose unit had no Payment for the month when the run reached it has exactly one attempt-1 Payment for the month: `Paid`, `Failed`, or `Pending` if the processor was unavailable. A unit that already has a Payment for the month (`Paid`, `Pending` or `Failed`, of any method) gets no new attempt-1 Payment: a `Pending` one is handled by D26 and D25, and a `Failed` one by R5 and D17. `ach` schedules are excluded under the settled OQ2 decision (D34).
- **R5 (postcondition):** For `today`'s billing_month, a unit whose month has no `Paid` or `Pending` Payment and whose latest attempt is `Failed`, has a `failed_on` date and is below MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS gets exactly one new attempt on the first run whose `today` is at least RETRY_DELAY_DAYS after that `failed_on` date, and none earlier (D17, D35). A retry date that falls in a later month gives the earlier month no retry (settled OQ3).

## 7. Edge cases

- The run is interrupted after the processor charged a unit but before its Payment was saved as `Paid`, then restarted.
- A manual payment recorded on the day of the run, or after a declined charge but before its retry.
- A schedule created after its chosen day this month; a `day_of_month` of 29 to 31; the processor unreachable.

## 8. Decisions and open questions

**Decisions**

- **D1:** `day_of_month` is 1 to 28, so every month has the chosen day. **D2:** The current month’s charge is the community’s dues_amount_cents; residents cannot enter an amount. Card fees are OQ1; earlier months are OQ3. **D3:** All money is integer cents.
- **D4:** Each attempt is saved as `Pending` with its key before the processor call, then set to `Paid` or `Failed` (for ACH, see OQ2). On restart a `Pending` Payment is charged again with the same key, so the processor returns its earlier result. **D5:** `today` is a UTC date passed to `run_due_payments`; every `created_at` comes from `app/clock.py::now()`, which tests replace.
- **D6:** S-1’s D1, D3, D6, D7 and D12 apply (identity, hidden records, check order, error format, storage). **D7:** Only a caller with the `resident` role in the community can create or read a schedule, and it is for their unit there. Any other caller gets `404 "Not found"` at the S-1 404 step, before the body checks (D22).
- **D8:** In the payments list a resident sees their own unit and a manager every unit they manage; admins, non-members and board members get `404 "Not found"` (board-wide visibility is deferred by the settled OQ4). All attempts are listed by unit_id, then attempt, then id (D23).
- **D9:** `month` is required and must be `YYYY-MM` with month 01 to 12, otherwise `422 "Invalid month"`; a month with no Payment records visible to the caller returns an empty list. `record_manual_payment` accepts any valid billing_month, including a future one, so a future month can have a Payment (Gap Check 2 C-03; behavior unchanged). **D10:** AC-18 is verified on the JSON response of `GET .../payment-schedules/mine`; no user interface is built. AC-18 was amended by human decision on 2026-11-23: its original wording required the display text “Scheduled: day 5 of each month, ACH”, which no part of the system produces, and it now requires the response to show `method` `"ach"`, `day_of_month` `5` and `active` `true`. “Payment history” in AC-19 to AC-22 means the response of `GET /communities/{community_id}/payments?month=<billing_month>` for R1, the resident of unit 12 (D8).
- **D11:** A schedule cannot be changed, paused or cancelled, so `409 "Schedule already exists"` applies to any existing schedule for the unit. **D12:** A missing field or a wrong type in any field (`method` not a string, `day_of_month` not an integer or a bool, `token` not a string) is `422 "Invalid request body"`; these type checks run for all three fields first, and then the values are checked in the order method, day_of_month, token (D21). Tokens are opaque and are not checked with Stripe when saved; with Stripe, a bad one would be declined at charge time (D33, not built). Tests and the demonstration use opaque test tokens such as `pm_card_visa`, which represent no real bank account; real ACH bank-account support is not built.
- **D13:** Manual payments are recorded only by `record_manual_payment`, which creates a `Paid` Payment or raises `AlreadyPaid` if the month is already `Paid`. **D14:** dues_amount_cents values are set by `app/seed.py`. **D15:** `app/settings.py` holds RETRY_DELAY_DAYS = `3` and MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS = `2`. They were first proposed in our proposal, not taken from interviews, and the human reviewer has approved them (see "Named settings" below); tests read the settings, never these values.
- **D16:** `ProcessorUnavailable` leaves the Payment `Pending` without using an attempt, and the next run retries the same key. `charged` and `failed` count Payments that became `Paid` or `Failed` in the run, retries included; `skipped` counts due schedules left unpaid because the month was `Paid` or the processor was unavailable. In the D26 replay phase, each replayed Payment that ends `Paid` or `Failed` is counted in `charged` or `failed`, and one left `Pending` by `ProcessorUnavailable` is counted in `skipped`, whatever its month and even when its schedule is not due. A due schedule whose month that run's replay settled as `Paid` is then counted again in `skipped` by the schedule pass, so one unit can be counted in both `charged` and `skipped` in one run. This is a known counting limitation (AGENT-03 F-08), not fixed; no test checks `skipped` after a replay. **D17:** A retry is due RETRY_DELAY_DAYS after the `today` of the run that made the failed attempt, for the same billing_month. After the last allowed attempt fails, nothing more happens that month; the schedule stays active.
- **D18:** A schedule is due on each run in `today`’s month from its day_of_month to the month’s end, so a missed run is caught up; D25 gives the exact test. Being due does not by itself create a Payment: D25 decides what the run does. A new schedule is first due in the first month whose chosen day is after its created_at date.
- **D19:** `python -m app.run_payments` calls `run_due_payments` with today’s UTC date; scheduling that command is out of scope. **D20:** `StripeTestProcessor` uses PaymentIntents in test mode (key in STRIPE_TEST_KEY); a card error is `declined`, and a connection error or 5xx raises `ProcessorUnavailable`. Tests use only `FakeProcessor`. processor_id is null while a Payment is `Pending` and is stored when it becomes `Paid` or `Failed`.

- **D21:** `method` must be exactly `ach`, `debit` or `credit` (case-sensitive; `manual` is "Unknown method"). `day_of_month` must be a JSON integer, not a bool, float or string, otherwise "Invalid request body"; an integer outside 1 to 28 is "Day must be 1 to 28". The token is not trimmed; an empty or whitespace-only token is "Token is required".
- **D22:** `POST` runs S-1's 401 and 404 checks, then the D12 body checks, then the 409 check. A unit has at most one schedule, whichever resident of it creates it. `GET .../mine` with no schedule returns `404 "Not found"`.
- **D23:** "Every unit they manage" means every unit of the requested community (the path `community_id`) when the caller has the `manager` role there; units of other communities the caller manages are not included. The list includes `Pending`, `Paid` and `Failed` Payments with the `PaymentOut` fields only, ordered by unit_id, then attempt, then id.
- **D24:** `app/clock.py::now() -> datetime` returns a timezone-aware UTC datetime; every `created_at` is formatted as in S-1's D5.
- **D25:** A run handles only active `debit` and `credit` schedules (`ach`: D34), and only the billing_month of `today` (earlier months are OQ3). A schedule is due when `today.day >= day_of_month` and the date with that day in `today`'s month is strictly after the schedule's `created_at` UTC date. For a due schedule the run does the first of these that applies: if the month is `Paid`, it skips the schedule; if there is no Payment, it makes attempt 1; if the latest attempt is still `Pending` after the D26 replay, it makes no new attempt; if the latest attempt is `Failed`, it applies D17.
- **D26:** At the start of every run, every `Pending` Payment from any month is charged again with its own key, its stored amount and the unit's current schedule token, and keeps its attempt number. A `Pending` Payment whose month already has a `Paid` Payment is left as it is (OQ5, settled). The replay does not take the unit-row lock; it relies on the single runner (D32) and on `finish_payment` changing only a `Pending` Payment (D35).
- **D27:** `record_manual_payment` raises `PaymentPending` and records nothing while the month has a `Pending` Payment (OQ5, settled: the scheduled charge stands), and raises `ValueError` for an unknown unit, an invalid billing_month, or a community with no dues_amount_cents (D30). When the month has both a `Paid` and a `Pending` Payment, it raises `AlreadyPaid`. When the month has only `Failed` attempts it records the payment. Its amount is the community's dues_amount_cents at that moment.
- **D28:** `app/payments.py` holds `run_due_payments`, `record_manual_payment`, `RunResult`, `AlreadyPaid` and `PaymentPending`. `app/processor.py` holds `Processor`, `ChargeResult`, `ProcessorUnavailable`, `FakeProcessor`, `StripeTestProcessor`, `get_processor() -> Processor` and `set_processor(p: Processor) -> None`; the default processor is `StripeTestProcessor`, and `run_due_payments` uses `get_processor()`.
- **D29:** `FakeProcessor(declined_tokens: frozenset[str] = frozenset(), unavailable: bool = False)` stores one result per key. A repeated key returns the stored result and charges nothing, even if the amount or token differs. `calls` lists every `(token, amount_cents, idempotency_key)` it received; a call is added to `calls` before anything else. A repeated key returns its stored result even when `unavailable` is true; otherwise an unavailable call raises `ProcessorUnavailable` and stores nothing. `declined_tokens` and `unavailable` are plain attributes that tests may change, and its processor_id values have the form `fake_<n>`.
- **D30:** A Payment's amount_cents is the dues_amount_cents at the moment the Payment is created; a charge that is retried after a restart uses the stored amount. `app/seed.py::set_dues(community_id: int, dues_amount_cents: int) -> None` raises `ValueError` unless the value is > 0. A community with no dues set has no due schedules.
- **D31:** The run keeps going after `ProcessorUnavailable` for one unit. Every change to a Payment is committed as soon as it is made. Any other exception stops the run and no RunResult is returned; this includes `RuntimeError` for a conflicting completion (D35) and `ValueError` for an unexpected processor status. This is how AC-20 simulates a crash.
- **D32:** The tables are `schedules` and `payments` in PostgreSQL (S-1 D12), with the §5 field names; `init_db()` runs `ALTER TABLE communities ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS dues_amount_cents` and `ALTER TABLE payments ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS failed_on` (D35). There is a unique index on `idempotency_key` and a partial unique index on `(unit_id, billing_month)` where status is `Paid` (R1). The unit-row-locking transactions of a scheduled attempt and of `record_manual_payment` use READ COMMITTED, set before their first query. Only one run at a time is supported.
- **D33:** `StripeTestProcessor` charges currency `usd`, confirming at once and off-session, with the token as the PaymentMethod id. A `CardError` is `declined`; any other Stripe error stops the run. `python -m app.run_payments` reads STRIPE_TEST_KEY from the environment, exits with code 2 if it is missing, and otherwise prints `charged=N failed=N skipped=N` and exits 0.
- **D34:** Under the settled OQ2 decision, ACH payments are not settled automatically. The tests for AC-19 to AC-22 use `debit` schedules, and nothing is built that decides how a successful ACH charge is shown. `ach` schedules can be created and shown (AC-18), but automatic ACH charging is not built, so `run_due_payments` creates and replays no `ach` Payment and does not count `ach` schedules in RunResult. Operationally, a unit with an `ach` schedule is never charged by the run and the run gives no signal; once its ACH funds have cleared, its dues can be recorded only with `record_manual_payment`. Scope confirmed by human decision on 2026-11-23: creating and viewing ACH schedules is in scope; automatic ACH charging and settlement are deferred. The opaque test tokens used for ACH schedules are not real bank accounts (D12).
- **D35 (AC-22, approved in AGENT-03 Prompts 55 to 58):** Payment has one more field, `failed_on` (§5): the `today` of the run in which that attempt became `Failed` (this is D17's "run that made the failed attempt"), and null for every other Payment. `init_db()` adds it with an additive, repeatable `ALTER TABLE payments ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS failed_on`; existing rows are kept and get null. A retry, attempt N for 2 <= N <= MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS, is created only when the month has no `Paid` or `Pending` Payment, its latest attempt (ordered by attempt, then id) is N - 1 and `Failed` with a `failed_on` date, and `today` is at least RETRY_DELAY_DAYS after that date; a `Failed` Payment with null `failed_on` is never retried automatically. The run re-checks all of this under the same unit-row lock as `record_manual_payment` (AGENT-03 F-01) and saves the retry as `Pending` with key `{unit_id}:{billing_month}:{N}` before calling the processor (D4). A new retry's amount is the dues at its creation (D30); a replayed `Pending` retry uses its stored amount (D26). Waiting and exhausted schedules are not counted in RunResult (D16). Retries happen only for `today`'s billing_month (D25): a failed attempt whose retry date falls in a later month is never retried, and the later month starts again at attempt 1 (OQ3, settled: earlier months are handled manually). `finish_payment` changes only a `Pending` Payment, so a completed Payment is never overwritten (AGENT-03 F-11). If a processor result arrives for a Payment that is no longer `Pending`, the run reads its stored status: if it matches the result, nothing changes and the completion is not counted again in RunResult; if it differs, the run stops with an error (D31) and the stored Payment is kept. A conflicting completion persists no conflict marker: the stored Payment keeps its status and `failed_on`, so if D32 is violated and the processor had in fact charged a Payment stored as `Failed`, a later run could still retry it and create a duplicate external charge. This is an accepted, unresolved residual risk under D32 (Prompt 58) and is recorded for the human ledger entry.

**Step 1 review clarification (not a new decision):** D4/D29/D31 specify a stable idempotency key and persisted `Pending` state. The AC-20 test verifies that the restarted run leaves exactly one Payment for the month, `Paid`, and that every processor call used a single idempotency key. The AGENT-03 F-03 test also verifies that the `Pending` Payment exists when the processor is called and that the restart reuses its key and stored amount without a new attempt or row. Both tests keep the same fake processor object after an injected exception, so neither verifies an actual process restart or an independently durable external processor result. D32 supports a serialized runner only; the partial unique index does not establish safe concurrent orchestration. Since AGENT-03 F-01 and AC-22, a run's scheduled attempt, whether attempt 1 or a retry (D35), and `record_manual_payment` lock the same unit row and re-check the month's Payments before inserting, and the lock is released before the processor is called; both transactions use READ COMMITTED, set explicitly before their first query (D32). This enforces D25 and D27 when a manual payment overlaps a run and does not decide OQ5. The F-01 and AC-22 race tests simulate the overlap within one process, not with concurrent database connections, and none of this removes the D32 single-runner limitation.

**Open questions (settled for Milestone 2)**

Each question below is settled by a policy decision that the human reviewer approved before Prompt 65. In Prompt 65, Claude Code only checked the decisions against the existing code and tests, and the AGENT-04 patch recorded them here; the approval itself is recorded in LEDGER.md. Interview evidence (E entries) and open interview questions (Q entries) are cited only where they informed a decision; no interviewee approved these policies. D2, D4, D8, D25, D26, D27, D34 and D35 refer to these answers, and the behavior those decisions describe is unchanged.

- **OQ1:** Who pays the card processing fee, the resident or the association (Q-1)? **Settled (human policy decision):** the association absorbs card-processing fees. A resident is charged exactly the community's dues_amount_cents, with no fee added (D2, AC-19). Source: team policy decision approved by the human reviewer (checked by Claude Code in Prompt 65); Q-1 remains unanswered by interviewees.
- **OQ2:** Should an ACH payment show as `Paid` before the bank’s return window of 3 to 5 business days has passed (E-10)? **Settled (human policy decision):** no. ACH payments are not settled automatically: `run_due_payments` creates, replays and counts no `ach` Payment (D34), while `ach` schedules can still be created and shown (AC-18). A cleared ACH payment is recorded manually with `record_manual_payment` (D13, D27); the system does not itself check whether ACH funds have cleared. Source: interview evidence E-10 (a returned ACH payment is reported 3 to 5 business days later) informed the decision; the policy is a team decision approved by the human reviewer (checked by Claude Code in Prompt 65).
- **OQ3:** When a resident owes earlier months, should the scheduled payment charge only the current month? **Settled (human policy decision):** yes. A run handles only `today`'s billing_month (D25), and a failed attempt whose retry date falls in a later month is not retried (D35). Earlier unpaid months are handled manually, with `record_manual_payment` for the earlier billing_month. Source: team policy decision approved by the human reviewer (checked by Claude Code in Prompt 65).
- **OQ4:** Should board members see every unit’s payments (S-8)? **Settled (human policy decision):** deferred. Board-wide payment visibility is not built in this release, and the D8 access rule stays in effect: a caller whose role in the community is `board_member` gets `404 "Not found"` from the payments list. Board reporting is left to S-8. Source: team policy decision approved by the human reviewer (checked by Claude Code in Prompt 65).
- **OQ5:** If a manual payment is recorded while that month’s scheduled charge is `Pending`, which stands, and is the other refunded? **Settled (human policy decision):** the scheduled charge stands. While the month has a `Pending` Payment, `record_manual_payment` raises `PaymentPending` and records nothing (D27). Once the run settles the charge, a manual payment can be recorded only if the charge ended `Failed`; if it ended `Paid`, `record_manual_payment` raises `AlreadyPaid` (D13). A `Pending` Payment in a month that already has a `Paid` Payment is left as it is (D26). The system makes no refunds, and this decision sets no refund policy for money received outside it. Source: team policy decision approved by the human reviewer on the condition that it matches the existing behavior of `record_manual_payment`; Claude Code confirmed that match against the code and tests in Prompt 65.

**Named settings (values approved by the human reviewer; human decisions, not interview evidence)**

- RETRY_DELAY_DAYS = `3` calendar days (`app/settings.py`; D15, D17, R5). Source: team decision approved by the human reviewer; first proposed in the team's proposal, not taken from interviews.
- MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS = `2` total attempts, the first charge plus one retry (`app/settings.py`; D15, D17, R5). Source: team decision approved by the human reviewer; first proposed in the team's proposal, not taken from interviews.
- DUES_AMOUNT_CENTS = `18500` for Mountain Ridge, as demonstration data only (`Community.dues_amount_cents`, set by `app/seed.py::set_dues`; D14, D30; AC-19). Source: team decision approved by the human reviewer; it is not an interview-confirmed amount. It matches the sample value `DUES_CENTS` used by the S-6 tests. Each real community's dues are set separately with `set_dues`.

**Known limitations and deferred items (scope confirmed by human decision on 2026-11-23; Gap Check 2 IDs in brackets)**

- Stripe: `StripeTestProcessor` is a placeholder, so D20 and D33 are not built or verified, including the mapping of Stripe errors to `declined`, `ProcessorUnavailable` or a stopped run, and intents that need further action [C-06, C-07, C-08, P-04, U-03]. Idempotency-key expiry and namespacing across environments are not handled [P-01, P-02, U-02].
- Command line: `python -m app.run_payments` (D19, D33) and its exit codes are not built [G-20, P-10].
- Concurrency: only one run at a time is supported (D32), and nothing enforces it [P-03]. The D26 replay is not locked [G-14]. The race tests run in one process, not with concurrent connections [U-04]. The duplicate-charge residual risk of D35 remains [C-05, U-08].
- Payment tokens: tokens are stored as plain text with no length limit or protection policy [P-06]. Tests use opaque test tokens; real ACH bank accounts are not supported (D12, D34) [G-02].
- Manual payments: `record_manual_payment` has no endpoint, authorization rule, payment-type field or audit trail [P-05]. Recording cleared ACH payments and earlier months depends on a person outside the system [U-07].
- Notifications: nothing tells the resident or manager that retries are exhausted or that an ACH schedule is never charged (S-7 is a non-goal) [P-07].
- Refunds: no refund policy is set (OQ5) [P-09].
- Counting: one unit can be counted in both `charged` and `skipped` in one run (D16, AGENT-03 F-08) [G-11].
- Restart: AC-20's test simulates a crash with the same fake processor and does not restart a process [U-01].
- Minor storage details are not specified beyond the code: column types and the locked row in `units` [G-21].
